Assess and guard your AI models and agents

AI Assurance is an AI risk assessment and guardrails platform. It finds the risks in computer vision models, large language models, vision-language models and AI agents, puts guardrails in front of them, and measures what those guardrails stop on the same attacks.

Three things

What sets our platform apart?

01

Backed by rigorous research

We have developed advanced red-teaming and guardrail algorithms in-house. NesyGuard, our neuro-symbolic guardrail for agents, is one of them.

NesyGuard in practice

It seals a contract from the user's instruction before the agent reads any tool output, then checks every tool call against it.

02

Fast and cost-effective

We draw on our expertise in high-performance computing to give our users solutions that keep both the cost and the latency low.

In practice

Open models run on your own GPU workstations, started when needed and stopped when idle, and independent model calls run side by side.

03

Tailored to real application needs

We build it in close collaboration with domain experts, so the tests and the guardrails fit what users actually face.

In practice

Agents are tested on realistic work, such as banking, office, team chat and travel tasks, where one injected instruction could do real harm.

Built for organisations that deploy AI

We work with customers and collaborators who need evidence that their AI is safe, both before it goes live and while it runs.

Public agencies

Assess AI services before they reach the public, keep protected data on approved infrastructure, and keep an audit trail of every guardrail decision.

Financial services providers

Test agents that handle accounts and payments against injected instructions, and check every tool call before it can move money.

Healthcare solution providers

Check how models answer questions about medical images and whether they treat patient groups fairly, while patient data stays away from public cloud APIs.

And more

Any team that builds on detectors, language models, vision-language models or agents, and research partners who want to take a new attack or guardrail from paper to practice.

What the platform helps you do

Three jobs, from the first test of a model to the decision to release it.

Find the risks

Test a model or an agent on benchmarks and attacks: robustness, safety, privacy, fairness, and utility under attack.

For ML engineers and developers, AI researchers and evaluators

Stop the attacks

Put a guardrail in front of the model, then measure what it stops — and what it costs in time — without and with it.

For AI security and red teams, MLOps and platform teams

Show the evidence

Open the answer behind any score, compare models side by side, and export a report or read the audit log.

For risk and compliance officers, auditors and regulators

One platform, four workspaces

Each workspace has its own datasets, models, runs, leaderboard and guardrails, behind one login.

Computer vision model

Image in, detections out

Assess
How far does detection quality drop under blur, weather-like corruptions and adversarial attacks?
Guard
Screening of adversarial images, input purification including a RAW camera-pipeline defence, and robust inference.

Large language model

Text in, text out

Assess
How accurate, safe and privacy-preserving is a model on curated benchmarks, from hallucination to jailbreak and privacy tests?
Guard
A guardrail screens every prompt and answer, with jailbreak screening, block keywords and PII redaction from your policy.

Vision-language model

Text and image in, text out

Assess
Does the model read images correctly, treat demographic groups fairly, and resist jailbreaks hidden in a picture?
Guard
A guardrail reads the picture together with the prompt, alongside jailbreak screening and PII redaction on the text.

Agentic system

Text and tools in, text and actions out

Assess
Do tool-using agents finish their tasks in banking, office, team chat and travel domains, and can injected instructions hijack them?
Guard
NesyGuard checks every tool call against a contract sealed from the user's instruction, and redacts an answer that leaks.
  • Privacy-aware routing. A private dataset never reaches a public cloud API: every dataset and model pair is checked before a run and again before each model call.
  • Guardrails you can operate. Every workspace tries its guardrail on a test bench, benchmarks it without and with it, and serves it through an API with a policy editor and an audit log.
