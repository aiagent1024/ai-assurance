Assess and guard your AI models and agents

AI Assurance is an AI risk assessment and guardrails platform. It finds the risks in computer vision models, large language models, vision-language models and AI agents, puts guardrails in front of them, and measures what those guardrails stop on the same attacks.

The challenge

Testing alone, or guarding alone, leaves a gap

Most teams have one half of the picture. Each half on its own leaves risks open.

01 · Assessment only

A benchmark score is a snapshot

It shows where a model failed on the day it was tested. It does not stop the next attack once the model is live.

02 · Guardrails only

An unmeasured guardrail is a guess

Without trying it on real attacks, nobody knows what a guardrail stops, what it lets through, or how much time it adds.

03 · Generic tests

General test sets miss your context

An agent that moves money, or a model that reads medical images, faces risks that general-purpose tests do not cover.

AI Assurance joins the two halves. The attacks that find a weakness are the same attacks that measure the guardrail you put in front of it.

How it works

From the first test to a guardrail you can defend

The same three steps in every workspace, from the first test of a model to the decision to release it.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Test a model or an agent on curated benchmarks and attacks: robustness, safety, privacy, fairness, and utility under attack. Start in the playground, then submit full runs.

    • A leaderboard per workspace
    • The answer behind every score
    • Two models compared side by side

    For ML engineers and developers, AI researchers and evaluators

  2. 02

    Guard

    Put a guardrail in front, try it on one query in the test bench, then benchmark it on the same attacks — once without it and once with it.

    • Attacks resisted, without and with the guardrail
    • The time the guardrail adds
    • A policy editor for what it refuses

    For AI security and red teams, MLOps and platform teams

  3. 03

    Prove

    Serve the guardrail through an API and keep the evidence that reviewers, auditors and regulators ask for.

    • A Guard API with per-user tokens
    • An audit log of every decision
    • PDF reports to share with reviewers

    For risk and compliance officers, auditors and regulators

The platform

One platform, four workspaces

Each workspace has its own datasets, models, runs, leaderboard and guardrails, behind one login and with the same pages.

Computer vision model

Image in, detections out

How far does detection quality drop under blur, weather-like corruptions and adversarial attacks?

Assess

  • Detection quality on clean images, then under corruptions such as blur, noise and weather-like effects, at several severities
  • Adversarial attacks crafted against object detectors
  • Per-image results, with predicted boxes drawn on every picture

Guard

  • Screening that flags or refuses adversarial images
  • Input purification, including a RAW camera-pipeline defence
  • Robust inference, benchmarked without and with each defence

Large language model

Text in, text out

How accurate, safe and privacy-preserving is a model, and can an attacker talk it out of its rules?

Assess

  • Curated benchmarks for accuracy, hallucination and privacy
  • Jailbreak attacks: prompt injection, role-play, context poisoning and multi-turn jailbreaks
  • Every prompt and answer kept, with a judge's rating

Guard

  • A guardrail that screens every prompt and every answer
  • Jailbreak screening and block keywords from your policy
  • Detection and redaction of personal data

Vision-language model

Text and image in, text out

Does the model read images correctly, treat demographic groups fairly, and resist jailbreaks hidden in a picture?

Assess

  • Question answering on images, including medical images
  • Fairness of answers across demographic groups
  • Jailbreaks carried by a picture, such as text drawn into an image

Guard

  • A guardrail that reads the picture together with the prompt
  • Jailbreak screening and redaction of personal data in the text
  • A Guard API that accepts an image with each request

Agentic system

Text and tools in, text and actions out

Do tool-using agents finish their tasks, and can an instruction hidden in a tool's output hijack them?

Assess

  • Agents at work in banking, office assistant, team chat and travel planning domains
  • Six kinds of injection attack, hidden in tool outputs such as emails, files and web pages
  • Tasks completed with no attack and under attack, and the share of attacks resisted

Guard

  • NesyGuard seals a contract from the user's instruction before any tool output is read
  • Every tool call is checked against it, and a leaking answer is redacted
  • A session API your own agent can call before each tool call

Guard pages in every workspace

  • Test bench try a guardrail on one query
  • Benchmark run it over whole datasets
  • Impact without versus with it
  • API access call it from your code
  • Policy set what it refuses
  • Audit log every decision, kept

Deploy your way

Runs where your data is, and plugs into what you have

  • Your own infrastructure

    The platform can run on your servers, and open models are served on your own GPU workstations.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions organisations ask before they start.

Which kinds of AI can AI Assurance assess?

Four: computer vision models (object detectors), large language models, vision-language models and tool-using AI agents. Each has its own workspace with benchmarks, attacks and guardrails.

Can our data and models stay on our own infrastructure?

Yes. The platform can run on your own servers, and open models can be served on your own GPU workstations. A routing guard checks every dataset and model pair, so a private dataset never reaches a public cloud API.

Do we have to change our model or agent?

No. Models are connected through their endpoints. For agents you build yourselves, NesyGuard is available as a session API: seal a contract from the user's request, then check each tool call before it runs.

How do you measure a guardrail?

We run the same attacks on the same tasks twice, once without the guardrail and once with it. You see the share of attacks resisted in each case, and how much time the guardrail adds.

What evidence do we get?

Every score opens the prompt, answer or tool call behind it. Results go to leaderboards and PDF reports, and every decision the Guard API makes is kept in an audit log.
